IRON LOG
Iron Log is a personal training tracker — warm-ups, working sets, weight progression, streaks, and a full workout history, all in one place.
Why sign in? Your workout data is tied to your Google account so it's saved to the cloud and syncs across every device you use — sign in on your phone at the gym, check your progress later on a laptop. Nothing is shared publicly, and only you can see your logs.
